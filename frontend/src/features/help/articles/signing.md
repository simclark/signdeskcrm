# Signing experience

This page is for **recipients** who receive a SignDesk email, and for senders explaining the flow to clients.

Signing happens on a secure link — usually `…/sign/…` — and does **not** require a SignDesk workspace login.

## Signer flow

1. Open the link from the **Signing invitation** (or reminder) email.
2. Read the **Electronic records and signatures disclosure**.
3. Check consent and continue.
4. **Adopt a signature** — draw or type (first time / when required).
5. Complete assigned fields on the document (signature, initials, dates, text, checkboxes).
6. **Submit**, or **Decline** if you will not sign.

When the envelope finishes (all required signers done), downloads for the **signed PDF** and **Certificate of Completion** may be available from the completion screen (and always from the sender’s envelope detail).

## CC recipients

If you are marked **CC**, you typically receive a copy notice and can view the document. You are not asked to complete signature fields.

## Sequential vs parallel

- **Sequential** — you may not receive your invite until earlier signers finish.
- **Parallel** — all signers can be invited at the same time.

## If the link does not work

| Situation | What to try |
|---|---|
| Link expired or envelope expired | Ask the sender to **Resend** or send a new envelope |
| Envelope voided | Contact the sender; signing is closed |
| Already completed | You may see a completion / download view instead of fillable fields |
| Wrong person | Do not forward casually; ask the sender to update recipients |

## Privacy and consent

Consent to electronic records and signatures is recorded for the Certificate of Completion. Use a device and browser you trust. Do not share your personal signing link.

Senders configure disclosure text, reminders, and expiration under [Settings → E-signature](settings.md#e-signature).
