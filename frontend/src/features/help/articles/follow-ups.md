# Follow-ups

**Follow-ups** are manual outreach tasks for contacts who need a call, email, or check-in — especially when they are not ready to sign yet.

Open **Follow-ups** in the left nav, or schedule from a [Contact](contacts.md) detail page.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Still to do |
| **Done** | Completed |
| **Cancelled** | No longer needed |

## Schedule a follow-up

1. Open a contact.
2. Choose **Schedule follow-up**.
3. Set due timing and notes.
4. Find the task later under **Follow-ups** and mark **Complete** when finished.

## When to use follow-ups vs follow-up plans

| Use | Tool |
|---|---|
| One-off personal outreach | **Follow-ups** (this page) |
| Automatic emails after an envelope stalls, is declined, or completes | [Follow-up plans](follow-up-plans.md) |

## Tips

- Put the next action in the notes (“Call about disclosure questions”).
- Review open follow-ups daily alongside **Awaiting others** on the [Dashboard](dashboard.md).
- Completing a task does not change envelope status; finish signing work on the envelope itself.
