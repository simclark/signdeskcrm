# Contacts

**Contacts** are people you work with — clients, counterparties, and colleagues you track in the light CRM. Linking contacts to envelopes keeps history and follow-ups in one place.

Open **Contacts** in the left nav.

## List and add

1. Open **Contacts**.
2. Choose **Add contact** (or equivalent create action).
3. Enter name, email, phone, title, company, stage, tags, and notes as needed.

## Stages

Use stage to reflect where the relationship sits:

| Stage | Typical use |
|---|---|
| **Lead** | Early interest |
| **Active** | Working relationship |
| **Under contract** | Deal or agreement in progress |
| **Closed** | Finished successfully |
| **Inactive** | No longer engaged |

Stages are for your team’s organization; they do not change signing by themselves.

## Contact detail

On a contact you can:

| Action | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Edit** | Update profile fields, company link, tags, notes |
| **Schedule follow-up** | Create a task on [Follow-ups](follow-ups.md) |
| **New envelope** | Start an [envelope](envelopes.md) with this person in mind |
| Review **activity** | Notes and timeline of outreach |
| Review **related envelopes** | Packets tied to this contact |

## Tips

- Prefer real emails — signing invites and reminders go there.
- Link a [Company](companies.md) when the person represents an organization.
- Use tags for filters (market, source, agent name).
- Schedule a follow-up when someone is not ready to sign yet instead of only relying on envelope reminders.
