# Getting started

This page walks you from first signup to a usable workspace.

## Create a workspace

1. Open [https://signdeskcrm.com](https://signdeskcrm.com) (or your marketing site URL).
2. Choose **Start free workspace** (or go to `/signup`).
3. Enter:
   - **Company name** — display name for your organization
   - **Subdomain** — the short slug that becomes `https://your-slug.signdeskcrm.com`
   - Your name and work email
4. Check your inbox for a confirmation email. Open the link on your workspace subdomain, set a password, and you land in the app as **Owner** at `/app`.

Bookmark your workspace URL. Teammates and signers use the same subdomain for app login and signing links.

## Log in

- From your workspace host (`https://your-slug.signdeskcrm.com`), open **Log in** and sign in with email and password.
- From the marketing site, you may be asked for your **workspace** (subdomain) as well as credentials.

### Forgot password

1. On the login page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email.
3. Open the reset link from your inbox and set a new password.

## Invite your team

Owners and admins can invite people under **Settings → Members**.

1. Open **Settings** (left nav — visible to Owner and Admin only).
2. Open the **Members** tab.
3. Choose **Invite a member**.
4. Enter their email and role:
   - **Member** — day-to-day access (envelopes, CRM, templates)
   - **Admin** — same as Member, plus Settings
5. They receive an invitation email (expires after 7 days).
6. They open the invite link, set a password, and join the workspace.

See [Roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md) for what each role can do.

## Set up the workspace (recommended)

Before sending real documents, owners/admins should review:

| Tab | Why |
|---|---|
| **Workspace** | Company name, address, primary contact |
| **Branding** | Accent color, icon, logo (shown to your team and in emails) |
| **Email** | Subjects and body copy for invitations and reminders |
| **E-signature** | Timezone, expiration, reminders, support contact, ESIGN disclosure |
| **Members** | Invite teammates |

Details: [Settings](settings.md).

## Optional: Prefill records (Listings)

If you want property- or deal-style records that fill document fields automatically:

1. **Settings → Workspace → Modules**
2. Turn on **Prefill records**
3. Use **Listings** in the left nav to add or import records

Details: [Listings / Prefill records](listings.md).

## Your first useful actions

1. Open **Help** in the left nav anytime you need a walkthrough of a screen.
2. Add a [Company](companies.md) and a few [Contacts](contacts.md).
3. Upload a PDF under [Documents](documents.md), or open [Templates](templates-and-form-library.md), create a template, and **Prepare** fields — or **Clone** a form from the Shared library.
4. Click **New envelope** in the header.
5. Prepare recipients and fields, then **Send for signature**.
6. Open the envelope to download the signed PDF and Certificate of Completion when finished.

Full walkthrough: [Envelopes](envelopes.md).

## Profile

Every user can open the avatar menu → **Profile** to update name or password. Email is read-only.
