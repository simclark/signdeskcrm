# Companies

**Companies** are organizations linked to your contacts — brokerages, buyers, sellers, vendors, and similar.

Open **Companies** in the left nav.

## What you can do

| Action | Description |
|---|---|
| **Add company** | Create a record with name, website, and notes |
| Open **detail** | See company info and linked contacts |
| Edit / update | Keep website and notes current |

## How companies relate to everything else

- Assign contacts to a company so your roster stays organized.
- Envelopes and follow-ups still center on people and documents; companies are the organizational layer.

## Tips

- Use the legal or trading name your team recognizes.
- Put mailing details that belong to the workspace itself under [Settings → Workspace](settings.md), not only on a company record.
